About this archive
Contract

Legal Tender archives Commonwealth contract notices published to AusTender under the Commonwealth Procurement Rules. Source data is Department of Finance open data (CC BY 3.0 AU). Values are committed at award, not amounts actually paid.

Approaches to market come from AusTender's current-notice feed and are available as an Atom feed filtered by a watchlist. An approach is not an award.
